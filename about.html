Our Story

One Team. One Promise: A Move Done Right.

Vital Movers started with a simple frustration - moving day in Nairobi was stressful, chaotic and full of surprises. We decided to do it properly: show up on time, handle every box with care and charge a fair, upfront price.

Today we help families and businesses across Nairobi move house, relocate offices and shift single heavy items - with friendly service and honest prices every single time.
